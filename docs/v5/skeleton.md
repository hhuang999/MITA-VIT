## 1. 研究故事速览

我们把 ViT
的注意力重新看成“慢权重为每张图临时写出的快权重网络”，并用这个视角完整分析一遍注意力：先读出它在看什么，再找出这种读法的问题，最后用视角推出的修正去检验和改进。

**暂定标题：** *Attention as Fast Weights: A Read–Diagnose–Correct Lens
for Analyzing Vision Transformers*

**三个贡献：**

1.  **一个视角：** 把预训练 ViT
    的注意力看成每张图写出的快权重网络，并用执行检验证明读出的神经元确实被使用。

2.  **一套由视角推出的分析方法：**
    读取名单与执行检验、三个前提的诊断、三种读法修正，配合扣除中心偏差的评估。

3.  **一轮完整的分析：** 在 DeiT-S、DeiT-B、DINO ViT-S 上走完闭环。

## 2. 论文骨架

### 摘要（模板）

Attention maps are the default view into ViTs, but they are hard to
verify. We revisit attention as a fast-weight network that the slow
weights write for each image, and use this lens to analyze pretrained
ViTs in one loop. **Read:** restricting every layer to the \[16\]
neurons each region reads keeps \[72/62/83\]% of predictions, against
\[9/4/30\]% for random sets; after removing centre bias, reading still
goes to objects. **Diagnose:** the lens exposes three premises behind
reading an attention map, and we find where each fails \[层与模型\].
**Correct:** three lens-derived corrections change \[指标\] by \[数值\],
against controls that \[结果\]; changing the model instead (registers,
top-k training) moves the attention budget but not to objects.
