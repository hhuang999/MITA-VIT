\paragraph{Object geometry.} In an exploratory analysis, the mean C3 enrichment gain over R0 falls from 0.092 to 0.002 between the smallest and largest foreground-area quartiles in DeiT-S, and from 0.165 to 0.008 in DeiT-B. With patch AUROC the corresponding gains are 0.024/0.026 and 0.042/0.046. The size gradient depends on the endpoint. Enrichment has less headroom for large objects (Appendix~\ref{app:explore}).
